# BLACK SHEEP TOWN ornate-specificity literary polish contract

## Objective

Reopen the frozen v1.0.0 prose locally for one narrow source-locked pass:
recover marked, specific, sensory, figurative, or deliberately elevated Japanese
diction where the current English is flatter than an English novelist could
reasonably defend from the source.

This is not a mandate to make every line more ornate. A plain Japanese line
remains plain. A marked Japanese line may receive marked English only to the
degree licensed by its exact context, narrator, speaker, and rhetorical
function.

## Authority

1. The final shipped Japanese in
   `whole_work_edit/adjudication/release_frozen_translation_rows.tsv` is the
   sole prose authority.
2. The frozen English is the baseline being audited, not an authority that
   earns retention by default.
3. Existing source-read reviews, voice and terminology ledgers, whole-work
   continuity records, and prior audit decisions remain binding constraints.
4. No previous English translation may be used as a drafting source or
   evidentiary witness.

## Positive revision test

A wording change may be accepted only when all of the following are true:

- the Japanese contains materially greater specificity, ornament, sensory
  force, rhetorical shape, or tonal elevation than the current English;
- the proposed English is idiomatic literary English that a working novelist
  could defend in the same register;
- the gain survives full-line and local-scene context rather than isolated
  dictionary substitution;
- the change preserves propositions, agency, modality, negation, aspect,
  referents, viewpoint, knowledge limits, ambiguity, repetition, and reveal
  timing;
- the change preserves character voice, narrator stance, terminology, proper
  names, markup, and deliberate oddness; and
- the change improves the line without requiring invented imagery,
  interpretation, psychology, causality, or emphasis.

## Mandatory rejection conditions

Reject a candidate when it:

- adds color that is merely plausible rather than present;
- converts a conventional Japanese collocation into conspicuously purple
  English;
- intensifies or sentimentalizes emotion;
- resolves ambiguity or upgrades inference into fact;
- substitutes a thesaurus word that is less natural in context;
- erases purposeful simplicity, repetition, fracture, awkwardness, or
  bathos;
- changes a locked term, name, quotation, tips/ruby mechanism, control tag, or
  line topology without a separate adjudication; or
- improves an isolated sentence at the expense of paragraph rhythm or voice.

## Review lanes

1. Marked lexeme and rhetoric screen.
2. Sensory and physical precision screen.
3. Manner, gesture, and affect screen.
4. Figurative-language and tonal-elevation screen.
5. English genericity and compression screen.
6. Continuous scene-context prosecution of every proposed change.
7. Post-application bilingual fidelity and English-only read.

## Output discipline

- The published v1.0.0 files remain untouched during review.
- Every accepted revision is logged by permanent `bst:` reference with source,
  previous English, proposed English, category, and evidence.
- Rejected candidates remain reviewable and do not alter prose.
- A new working TSV is materialized only from accepted decisions.
- No patch, website, tag, release, or remote branch is rebuilt or published
  without a later explicit instruction.
